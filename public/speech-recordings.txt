https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03209
Source subset:
https://storage.googleapis.com/download.tensorflow.org/data/mini_speech_commands.zip

This lesson includes 32 original one-second recordings as lossless PCM16 data.
Two anonymized dataset speakers are called Voice A and Voice B. For each word
and speaker, the first filename in sorted order is the reference recording;
the second is the separate input example. Selection was based on availability,
not recognition accuracy. During an experiment, the program adjusts gain,
optionally adds deterministic noise, and optionally filters and downsamples.
Those transformations are teaching experiments, not original recording conditions.
No endorsement by Google or the contributors is implied.
